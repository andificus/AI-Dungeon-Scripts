The darkness is not dark.

It has texture — weight — the particular quality of a silence that is listening. And somewhere inside it, you exist. Not yet a body. Not yet a name. Something in between.

"Welcome. You are... unexpected. The System has flagged your existence as an irregularity — something that should not be possible under the current world's laws. Before your story can begin, certain parameters must be established."

"Please respond to each prompt in turn. Your answers will define who you are — and what kind of legend you leave behind."
