# STORY CARD: Karma & Alignment

**Card Title:** Karma Alignment
**Trigger Keys:** karma, reputation, alignment, Divine Savior, Holy Champion, Demon Lord, Cataclysmic, feared, savior, hero's reputation, public opinion, wanted, bounty, revered

---

## Entry (paste this into the card's Entry field)

The world keeps a running account of who you are and what you have done. This is called Karma — not in a mystical sense, but as a practical measure of how the world perceives and responds to you.

**How Karma Works**
Every significant action leaves a mark. Saving a village, defeating a corrupt lord, or returning a kidnapped child pushes Karma toward the positive. Threatening civilians, working with criminal organizations, destroying infrastructure, or leaving people to die when intervention was possible pushes it negative. Karma accumulates invisibly and manifests visibly — in how guards address you at city gates, how merchants price their goods, and whether the church sends a letter of commendation or a tribunal summons.

**What Karma Affects**
- NPC reactions and willingness to help
- Prices in shops and services
- Guild mission availability and mission rewards
- Whether kingdoms offer contracts or issue warrants
- Church and noble house attitudes
- Whether underground networks treat you as a client or a target
- What songs the bards are singing about you in taverns

**High Positive Karma**
At high positive Karma, the world begins to treat you as a symbol. Strangers recognize your name. Children follow you through markets. Kingdoms send formal envoys rather than casual requests. The Church may attempt to canonize you while you are still alive, which most recipients describe as deeply inconvenient.

**High Negative Karma**
At high negative Karma, the world reacts with fear. Settlements may close their gates on your approach. Bounties accumulate. Assassination contracts become available on the black market — which tends to attract adventurers looking for a very large payday and a very short life expectancy. At the Cataclysmic Demon Lord tier, kingdoms begin discussing pre-emptive military action in diplomatic channels. At Apocalyptic God of Destruction, they stop discussing it and start drafting wills.
