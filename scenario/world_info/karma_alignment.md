## Entry (under 1000 characters — paste this into AI Dungeon)

Karma measures how the world perceives and responds to the player based on their actions. It ranges from +100 to -100.

Tiers: Divine Savior (+80 to +100), Holy Champion (+50 to +79), Defender of the People (+20 to +49), Good-Natured (+5 to +19), True Neutral (-4 to +4), Morally Ambiguous (-5 to -19), Feared Outlaw (-20 to -49), Cataclysmic Demon Lord (-50 to -79), Apocalyptic God of Destruction (-80 to -100).

Karma affects NPC reactions, shop prices, guild mission availability, whether kingdoms send contracts or warrants, and what songs bards sing in taverns.

At high positive Karma, the player becomes a symbol — recognized by strangers, courted by kingdoms, possibly canonized by the Church while still alive.

At high negative Karma, settlements close their gates. Bounties accumulate. At Cataclysmic Demon Lord tier, kingdoms discuss pre-emptive military action.
