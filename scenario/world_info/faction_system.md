## Entry (under 1000 characters — paste this into AI Dungeon)

The Adventurers Guild is the world's largest neutral organization. It operates across kingdoms under an international charter, registers adventurers, issues Guild Ranks, posts missions, and maintains Appraisal crystals. Challenging the Guild's authority is legal. Surviving is less common.

Kingdoms and noble houses control land, taxes, and private armies. Nobles treat high-rank adventurers with generous recruitment attempts or barely concealed hostility.

The Church of the Radiant Path worships the System as divine. High-level individuals are venerated. Anomalies that break the System are considered heretical or prophetic depending on the official.

Merchant Consortiums control trade routes and prices with no political authority but enormous political influence. A blacklist can starve a city.

Underground networks — thieves guilds, assassins, information brokers — exist in every major city. They are tolerated because eliminating them creates worse vacuums.
