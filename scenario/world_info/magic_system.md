# STORY CARD: Magic System

**Card Title:** Magic System
**Trigger Keys:** magic, spell, mana, casting, Vein Lines, elemental, sorcery, enchantment, conjure, incantation

## Entry (paste this into the card's Entry field)

Magic flows through invisible channels called Vein Lines — lattice-like networks that run beneath the skin of all living things. Most people are born with sealed Vein Lines and live their entire lives without accessing them. Awakening requires either a catalyst event, years of deliberate training, or a rare natural talent that manifests on its own.

**Elemental Affinities**
All magic aligns to one of eight primary elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Void, Light, and Shadow. A mage's affinity is set at the moment of awakening and cannot be changed. Dual affinities are exceptional and command enormous respect. Triple affinities are considered myth by most and documented fact by very few.

**Mana**
Mana is the fuel. It replenishes over time through rest, meditation, and proximity to ley lines. Overextension causes Vein Line strain — painful, degenerative, and in severe cases permanent. Mages who push past their limits are said to "burn their lines."

**Spatial Magic**
The rarest form. Spatial magic manipulates dimensional boundaries — storage rings, teleportation gates, pocket dimensions, and in the most extreme cases, dimensional severance. Spatial mages are so uncommon that most kingdoms register them as national assets upon identification. Spatial affinity combined with any combat element is considered a disaster-tier threat profile.

**Magic and the System**
The System quantifies magical ability as a stat (INT, WIS, and MP pool). However, raw System numbers do not capture affinity rarity, technique mastery, or creative application. A Level 12 spatial mage with clever application has historically defeated Level 60 fire mages who relied on brute output. The System measures potential. Skill determines outcomes.
