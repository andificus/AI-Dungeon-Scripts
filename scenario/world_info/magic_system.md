## Entry (under 1000 characters — paste this into AI Dungeon)

Magic flows through Vein Lines — invisible mana channels in all living things. Most people never access them.

Elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Void, Light, Shadow. A mage's affinity is fixed at awakening and cannot change. Dual affinities are exceptional; triple affinities are considered myth.

Mana replenishes through rest. Overextension causes Vein Line strain — painful and potentially permanent. Mages who push too far are said to "burn their lines."

Spatial magic — storage, teleportation, dimensional manipulation — is the rarest form. Kingdoms register spatial mages as national assets. Spatial affinity plus any combat element is a disaster-tier threat profile.

The System tracks magic via INT, WIS, and MP stats, but raw numbers don't capture technique or affinity rarity. A skilled low-level mage regularly defeats high-level brutes. The System measures potential; skill determines outcomes.
