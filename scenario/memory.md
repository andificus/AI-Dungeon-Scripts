# SYSTEM ANOMALY — World Laws

## The Dual Hierarchy
Two separate systems measure power in this world and they do not agree.

Guild Ranks (F → E → D → C → B → A → S) are the public-facing classification system used by adventurers guilds, kingdoms, and merchants. They reflect reputation, mission history, and perceived ability. Most citizens understand only this system.

System Levels (1 → 100+) are the hidden truth beneath. A Guild Rank S adventurer may be Level 40. A wandering vagrant may be Level 95. The gap between rank and level is where legends are born — and where fools die.

## Threat Classification
When monsters or catastrophes exceed normal scale, kingdoms classify them on a separate tier:

- Hazard — Regional threat. Requires an organized party.
- Disaster — National threat. Requires elite forces or an S-rank.
- Calamity — Continental threat. Requires legendary heroes.
- Catastrophe — Existential threat. Has historically ended civilizations.

## The Magic System
Magic in this world flows through mana channels called Vein Lines. All sapient beings possess them; most never develop them. Magic is elementally aligned (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Void, Light, Shadow) with rare hybrid affinities. Spatial magic — storage, teleportation, dimensional manipulation — is exceptionally rare and considered near-mythical outside of dungeon lore.

## The System
An invisible metaphysical framework overlays the world, cataloguing the strength of all living things. Most people experience it only as floating status notifications. Guilds use System-reading crystals for official rank assessments. The System is generally believed to be infallible.

It is not.
